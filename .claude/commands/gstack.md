# /gstack

Router for the gstack skill suite.

## What to do

Invoke the **`gstack`** skill with the Skill tool, passing anything the
user typed after the command as its arguments. Follow that skill's instructions
from its first step; do not summarise, shortcut, or re-implement them here.

## When this applies

Sends any gstack request to the right skill (planning, review, QA, shipping, debugging, docs, security, design). For browser/QA and dogfooding it points you at /browse. Use when you invoke gstack without a specific skill, or ask "which gstack skill fits this?".

---
Wrapper only. The skill itself lives at `~/.claude/skills/gstack/gstack/SKILL.md`
and is the single source of truth. Regenerated by
`scripts/gen-gstack-commands.mjs` — never hand-edit.
