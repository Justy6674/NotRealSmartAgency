  • ✓ No promise about results
  • ✓ No patient stories or testimonials
  • ✓ No before-and-after photos
  • ✗ Names a prescription medicine — four times
  • ✓ Says who wrote it and their qualification
  • ✓ No wording that pressures someone to book

What is wrong. The heading and three sentences name a prescription-only medicine. In Australia nobody may advertise one to the public — a clinic, a chemist or a writer. It is the line that carries a fine of up to $60,000 an offence, so we hold the post rather than let it through.

What to do. Write about appetite and treatment in general and leave the medicine unnamed. People searching this are asking how it feels, not which brand it is.

It cannot be marked ready until this passes. Nothing about this post has gone anywhere — it has only ever been here.
