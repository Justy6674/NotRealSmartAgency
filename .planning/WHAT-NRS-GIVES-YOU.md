# What NRS Gives You

**NRS is a marketing agency you own — a head marketer plus thirteen specialists who know your eleven businesses, write and design and publish for you, and check the health ones against AHPRA and TGA before anything goes out.**

That is what it is. What follows is what it actually does today, what it knows, what it does not do, and where the gap is between what you paid to build and what you are using.

Written 30 July 2026. Everything below was checked against the code and the live database, not against the docs. Where the docs were wrong, the docs are noted as wrong.

---

## 1. What you can ask it for today

You talk to NRS through Hermes, Claude Desktop or Claude Code. You type a sentence. It plans the work, hands it to the right specialists, and comes back.

One thing to know up front: **almost everything goes through one door.** You ask, it thinks, it replies. Most requests take 15 to 90 seconds. A handful of quick things (look at my calendar, look in my media library, scan a page) answer instantly. Everything that involves writing, planning or judgement goes through the head marketer.

### Get found on Google and in AI search

| Type this | What comes back |
|---|---|
| "Look at downscale.com.au and tell me what's wrong with it for search" | A real browser opens your site, reads the actual page a visitor sees, and reports titles, headings, missing descriptions, calls to action, forms, structured data, plus a screenshot |
| "What should I be writing about for weight management in Australia?" | Keyword clusters — head terms, long-tail, "[city]" local variants, question phrasing — built for AU health specifically |
| "Write me a blog post about NP-led weight management" | A full article with title, meta description, headings, internal link suggestions, saved to your library |
| "How do I get ChatGPT and Perplexity to mention Downscale?" | The FAQ structure, the schema markup, the quotable-fact formatting and the author credentials that get you cited by AI search |
| "Set up local search for the clinic" | Google Business Profile, name/address/phone consistency, the AU health directories, the right schema type |

**The honest limit:** it has no connection to Google Search Console, Google Analytics, SEMrush or Ahrefs. **It cannot tell you your real rankings, your real search volume or your real traffic.** It says so rather than making numbers up — that refusal is built in. So: excellent audit and strategy, zero ranking data. If you want rank tracking, that is a thing to build, not a thing to switch on.

### Know what competitors are doing

| Type this | What comes back |
|---|---|
| "Scan juniper.com.au and compare them to Downscale" | Their pricing, a feature-by-feature table, their positioning and tone, their headings and page titles, their social presence, and 3 to 5 specific recommendations |
| "What's happening in the Australian telehealth market?" | Live web research on trends, buyer psychology, what content is working |
| "Who should I be watching in medical skincare?" | A competitor list, saved into the brand so it compounds |

**The honest limit:** each scan reads **one page** of a competitor's site — the homepage unless you give it a different URL. It cannot see their ad spend, their traffic or their rankings. It is a sharp read of what they say publicly, not intelligence on how they are performing. Point it at their pricing page separately if you want their pricing properly.

### Plan the next 90 days

| Type this | What comes back |
|---|---|
| "What's my 90-day plan for TeleCheck?" | A structured plan stored permanently against that business, reviewed monthly |
| "I'm launching DownDiary — what do I do?" | A 12-week checklist split into pre-launch, launch week and post-launch, with named deliverables |
| "Build me a month of content for Scent Sell" | Real draft posts, placed in optimal Australian time slots, rotated across your content pillars, never double-booked, all sitting as drafts for you to approve |
| "What should I charge?" | Pricing analysis including cost to deliver, competitor pricing, and the Medicare rebate gap where it applies |
| "Review my brand" | Six specialists work in parallel — competitors, search, content, analytics, compliance, website — and you get one combined assessment |

The calendar filler is the strongest thing here. It works out which platforms you actually have, writes real captions in your brand voice, applies compliance rules for the health brands, and slots everything into weekday 9am/12pm/5pm and weekend 10am/2pm Australian time. Nothing publishes on its own.

### Write and publish content

| "Write me five Facebook ads for Downscale" | Multiple variants with the character limits enforced, not guessed — Meta 125/40/30, Google 30/90, LinkedIn, TikTok |

**Publishing reality:** everything goes out through your own Mixpost server — Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok. The "direct platform APIs" described in your notes are a folder of half-finished code that nothing calls. Every post you have ever published went through Mixpost.

### Make things look right

| Type this | What comes back |
|---|---|

| "Resize that design for LinkedIn and Stories" | The same graphic in every aspect ratio |

### Stay legal

This is the part that matters most, and it is genuinely built in code rather than just written into instructions.

| Type this | What comes back |
|---|---|
| "Can I say this?" | A line-by-line table: the exact phrase, the problem, the regulation, the risk level, and a compliant rewrite that keeps the marketing intent |
| "Check my website won't get me fined" | A compliance audit of what the site actually says |
| "Is this post safe to publish?" | A score out of 100 on voice, compliance and quality — it will not mark anything approved below 70 or with a critical issue |
| "What are my regulatory risks?" | Cited AHPRA and TGA answers, plus accreditation assessment against RACGP 6th ed, NSQHS 2nd ed, NDIS Practice Standards and Aged Care Quality Standards |

What it knows: no patient testimonials, no guaranteed outcomes, no superlatives without evidence, no before-and-after without consent and disclaimers, no inducements, no specialist title you do not hold (it explicitly knows never to write "Dr" for a nurse practitioner). Never name semaglutide, tirzepatide or liraglutide in consumer content. Never name tretinoin or adapalene. Never advertise prescription medicines to the public — you can advertise the service, never the drug. It carries the September 2025 update that AHPRA now scans websites and social media with AI, and that AI-written content carries identical legal liability.

**Two real holes you should know about:**

1. There is a second publishing route (Blotato) still switched on for the head marketer that **has no compliance check at all**. It cannot even see which brand it is posting for. Today's fix closed the main publishing route and the scheduled publisher — it did not close this one.
2. When you save a piece of work to your library, the compliance check runs, the verdict gets recorded — and **the work saves anyway, even if it failed**. That failed content then feeds back to the agents later as prior work.

Both are small fixes. Neither is fixed today.

## 2. What it knows about your business

Every business has a profile with 34 fields — name, site, positioning, voice, formality, humour, words to use, words never to use, audience demographics, pain points, desires, content pillars, products with prices and unique selling points, competitors with categories, brand colours, social handles, and a set of hard "never do this" rules marked permanent that override everything else.

Behind that sits a 21-section living plan per business, each section with a traffic light and a review clock: snapshot, client profile, brand fundamentals, audience, market, compliance, goals, funnel, website, search, social, paid, email, content, competitors, KPIs, **gaps and opportunities**, wins and losses, **risk register**, decision log, **90-day plan**.

That structure is exactly the macro view you have been asking for. Here is the honest state of it:

| | Number |
|---|---|
| Businesses in the system | 14 (11 active — your eleven) |
| Plan sections created | 252 |
| Sections showing red (empty) | 141 |
| Sections showing green | 16 |
| Sections ever reviewed or updated | **6 out of 252** |
| Things it has remembered | 441 |
| Site and social scans stored | 108 |
| Media files with transcripts and descriptions | 121 |
| Drafts written | 75 |
| Posts actually published | **8** |
| Posts cancelled | 22 |

The 21-section plan is a scaffold that has been filled in about six times. Gaps and Opportunities is empty on every business. The Risk Register has one auto-generated line ("AHPRA advertising compliance") on the health brands and nothing else. The 90-day plan is empty on all of them.

**It has deep hand-written knowledge of these:** Downscale Weight Loss, DownscaleDerm, TeleScribe, Tele360, TeleCheck, NotRealSmart, Downscale Diary, Scent Sell, Underground Parfums, EndorseMe.

**It has no written background on these four:** Do Today, Sniffopotamus, Black Health Intelligence, TeleCheck Clinic. For those it works from the basic profile only.

**Memory is split on Downscale.** Your biggest regulated brand has its memories filed under two different names, and only one of them can ever be read back. That is a bug worth fixing.

### The built-in marketing knowledge

Four reference libraries are compiled into the app and fed to the specialists:

- **Platform performance** — engagement benchmarks for seven platforms with direction of travel (Instagram at 5.4%, down from 7.3%), Australian dollar ad cost bands including a separate health band, video specs for eight surfaces, optimal posting times in Australian time, and what each platform's algorithm actually rewards.
- **Design intelligence** — exact pixel dimensions for thirteen surfaces, format performance rankings from a 52-million-post study, six carousel storytelling frameworks, four caption frameworks, accessibility requirements, and the fact that hashtags are dead for discovery.
- **Australian health marketing** — the regulations by section number, the named prohibited medicines, actual enforcement cases ($198,000 against a telehealth weight-loss advertiser), Google's and Meta's and TikTok's health advertising restrictions.
- **Your eleven businesses** — product, stack, practitioners, pricing, named competitors, compliance traps.

Two problems with the plumbing:

- **Your search specialist gets none of the platform knowledge.** It was left out of the routing list. Same for email, brand, website, compliance and automation.
- **Twenty deep knowledge entries — GLP-1 medications, the AHPRA advertising section, TGA advertising, NP endorsement, Medicare telehealth, skincare, clinical documentation — are locked in quarantine and cannot be read by anything.** They were written and seeded, then a security change locked them out. That is roughly 1,200 lines of your best regulatory writing that no agent can see.

## 3. What it does on its own

**Almost nothing. This is the honest answer and it has not been fixed.**

NRS does not start work. Every single thing it has ever done happened because you typed a message first. It will not look at a business, notice nothing has been posted in three weeks, and tell you.

Three things are scheduled to run:

| Runs | What it does |
|---|---|
| Every 5 minutes | Publishes posts that were already drafted, approved and scheduled. Creates nothing. |
| Every 15 minutes | Checks whether any business has a goal due for review. **If no goal exists, it does nothing.** No goal has ever been set — that table is empty. So this has been firing about 96 times a day into an empty queue. |
| Once daily | An intelligence email. Except it only sends if a specific configuration block was typed into your account settings, which was almost certainly never done. |

Three more engines are fully built and **have never run once**, because three lines are missing from one configuration file:

| Engine | What it would give you |
|---|---|
| **The watchdog** | Every hour, across all eleven businesses: which posts failed to publish, where you have nothing scheduled for the next three days, which platforms have gone silent for a week. There is already a red alert bar built into the Studio screen waiting to display this. |
| **The learning loop** | Every six hours, look at what actually got published, pull the real numbers, work out what performed, and write that back so the agents write better next time. You have 75 published drafts and **zero lessons learned from any of them**. |
| **Memory cleanup** | Weekly, throw away stale conversation transcripts and merge duplicate memories. Without it, memory only ever grows messier, which makes every answer slightly worse over time. |

The watchdog has a second problem — it writes to a column that does not exist, so even if you scheduled it tomorrow it would still record nothing. One word, misspelled, in two places. The alert bar reads the same wrong word.

**So the exact thing you keep asking for — "always suggest a plan, know my site, scan it, suggest optimisations, enablers, barriers, risks, gaps" — is precisely the part that has never run.** Not because it was not built. Because three entries are missing from a settings file, one word is misspelled, and nothing ever sets a goal for the engine to work towards.

## 5. What's genuinely missing

Short list. These are not switched-off features — they do not exist.

1. **A screen that shows all eleven businesses at once.** Right now the agency home page just redirects into chat. There is a finished business switcher sitting in the code that nothing uses. Seeing across all eleven takes about 23 clicks. The Mixpost-style or Canva-style macro view you keep describing is not built.

2. **Anything that speaks first.** There is no code path anywhere that looks at a business and raises a risk, a gap or a barrier without you asking. The instruction to "always suggest a plan" is written into the agent's personality — it is not a running process.

3. **Real numbers.** No Google Analytics, no Search Console, no rank tracking, no ad platform connections. The "how am I doing" tool counts your own drafts and reports what the AI cost. The one tool that reads real likes and reach goes through Mixpost's reporting screen, which the code itself already logs as not authenticating properly. Treat any engagement number as unverified.

4. **Memory that understands meaning.** The system was built to search memory by meaning, not just by matching words. That has never worked — the key it needs is not set, so every recall falls back to substring matching. Say "our voice" and it will not find a memory that says "our tone".

5. **A running record of what NRS has done.** Cost logging has been silently broken since launch — every write is rejected because it sends a field the table does not have. **Your costs screen showing $0 does not mean nothing was spent.** It means the meter never recorded anything. Same class of bug that cost a full session on the media library.

6. **Working memory across a conversation via Hermes.** Every message you send through Hermes is a blank slate apart from whatever memories get matched. It does not remember the last thing you said in that thread. Only the Telegram channel carries a conversation.

7. **Video generation.** Your notes list nine video tools — create a video, multi-scene video, text to speech, talking avatar, translation. **None of them exist.** They were removed. The video specialist writes scripts, transcribes recordings, repurposes them and publishes files. It cannot make a video.

## The one-paragraph version

You own a proper agency: fourteen specialists, around 97 tools, a real browser that reads your sites, a 21-section plan per business, compliance enforced in code for the health brands, publishing to five platforms, and the largest design capability in the system sitting mostly untouched. What you have actually used it for is writing captions — 75 drafts, 8 published. Everything before the caption (scanning, planning, goal-setting) and everything after it (approvals, real numbers, learning) is empty. Three engines that would make it proactive were built and never switched on. And it cannot tell you any of this itself, because the meter that would show you has been silently broken since day one.

The gap is not capability. It is plumbing — three settings entries, one misspelled word, two missing keys, and a screen that was never built.
